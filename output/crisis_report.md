# Crisis Response Report

## Executive Summary
We are facing multiple simultaneous disruptions across key global shipping lanes, with Typhoon Mawar (CRITICAL) and Shanghai Port Strike (HIGH) being the most urgent. Immediate action is required for 6 P1 shipments, particularly those in transit through Typhoon-affected waters. Top recommendations include rerouting Hong Kong-Los Angeles shipments via Manila/Honolulu (7 extra days), redirecting Shanghai-Hamburg cargo via Ningbo/Cape Town (12 extra days), and delaying Mumbai loadings until monsoon conditions improve. Estimated financial impacts range from $120,000 to $450,000 per delayed shipment.
